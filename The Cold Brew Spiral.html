Every summer, the cold brew spiral grows

This is a cup of coffee seen from above. The spiral starts at the center in January 2024 and winds outward, one petal per month, to December 2025 at the rim. Each petal's length is that month's revenue. Watch cold brew swell every summer, and reach further in 2025 than in 2024.
